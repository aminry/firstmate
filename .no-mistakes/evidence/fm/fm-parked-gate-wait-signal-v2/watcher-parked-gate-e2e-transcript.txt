END-TO-END WATCHER TRANSCRIPTS

Each scenario stands up an isolated firstmate home (state/, config/, a real throwaway
git worktree) and runs the REAL bin/fm-watch.sh against the REAL bin/fm-crew-state.sh,
which reads a CAPTURED no-mistakes v1.70.1 gate payload through a fake no-mistakes CLI.
Only the terminal backend (tmux) and the no-mistakes CLI transport are stand-ins.
FM_STALE_ESCALATE_SECS=1 puts every poll at the wedge threshold.

NOTE: two batch-1 entries are superseded. armed-cadence-absorb was mis-specified (a
fresh lane always surfaces its FIRST recheck; batch 2's armed-long-cadence covers the
cadence properly), and unarmed-home lost its summary when its driver file was edited
mid-run; it is re-run in batch 3.
